**Headline:** Fixed-512-token generation gets faster with MTP, but **a fresh 61,789- or 126,253-token prompt is slower end-to-end**. Context-dependent TG gains shrink from 64k to 128k as the MTP overhead and decode state length grow. Both ON PP rates are lower by ~6.9%, consistent across repetitions.

**Approximate illustrative break-even only:** If the observed PP overhead and the *measured 512-token effective TG time/token* remained constant for much longer outputs, the generation saving would amortize PP at roughly **1,347 output tokens (64k)** or **6,563 tokens (128k)**. This is **not a measured break-even**: draft acceptance, KV length, GPU residency, generation behavior and outputs change with length; do not assume linearity or an actual advantage at those lengths.

### Unexpected load-time outlier: 64k B2

The 64k B2 ON invocation took **376.429 seconds of process wall time** versus **231.512 seconds** for 64k B1 ON, although the respective native PP+TG times were **189.173 vs 188.213 seconds**. In B2 stderr, an elapsed-log timestamp jumps from **`0.01.784.534`** at `load_tensors: ROCm_Host model buffer size` to **`2.59.998.526`** at `common_init_ ... logit bias`: **~178.214 s**. B1 at the same stage takes **~34.258 s**. The extra **~144s** happened around **weight loading / model initialization**, **not** at FA/PP/TG. The cause is **not established** (possible external/storage/memory delay, do not assert which). System resources were monitored, but no unambiguous failure/error explains the outlier. Exclude this from comparative PP/TG attribution; preserve it as a real start-to-finish latency anomaly.

### Memory, resource monitoring and output caveats

- Approximate **process GPU Local peak** sampled by Windows resource logger: 64k OFF **52.964 GiB** vs ON **58.064 GiB**; 128k OFF **55.475 GiB** vs ON **60.895 GiB**. These are **Windows counters**, not direct physical UMA allocation measurements. MTP adds roughly **5.1–5.4 GiB** GPU-local accounting at these depths.
- Approximate system-wide committed memory peaks in the same sessions: 64k OFF **91.27–91.55 GiB**, ON **97.76–97.78 GiB**; 128k OFF **93.81–93.86 GiB**, ON **100.64–100.65 GiB**. Do not interpret as model-only commitment.
- Generated output **differs across individual runs, even with `--temp 0 --seed 1234`**. The ON and OFF outputs are not byte-identical; the two ON repetitions have draft acceptance differences (~2.3 pp at 64k, ~2.5 pp at 128k). Neither forced-reject checkpoint smoke nor bitwise target/MTP output-logit equivalence was run in this matrix. Acceptance variation is a confounder for TG and no quality/correctness equivalence is claimed from successful completion alone.
- `graphs reused` is **~205–209** on MTP ON vs **507** on OFF, reflecting different speculative graph workload counts; it is **not by itself proof of a graph-cache regression**. The r4 draft dense-indexer omission and target no-op suppression remain unported.
